Welcome!

I am a PhD candidate in Economics and Finance at the University of Trento, in a joint program with the Free University of Bozen-Bolzano. I am supervised by Matteo Ploner and Alessandro Fedele, and I am an affiliate of the Cognitive and Experimental Economics Laboratory (CEEL) at the University of Trento.

My research interests are in Behavioural and Experimental Economics.
